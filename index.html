Welcome to AnkaVeda ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and ensures transparency regarding data collection and usage.

1Information We Collect

AnkaVeda is designed to function primarily without collecting personally identifiable information (PII). Here is how specific data is handled:

  • Birth Dates and Numbers Entered: Any numbers (such as phone numbers or general numbers) and dates of birth entered into the app are processed locally on your device to compute numerological analyses and fortune calculations. We do not sell or link this data to your personal identity.
  • Locally Stored Preferences: Information such as your saved birth date is stored strictly on your local device using secure local storage (SharedPreferences) for your convenience, enabling quick loading of your personalized daily energy.

2Third-Party Services and Cloud Infrastructure

To provide high performance and reliable fortune insights, our application utilizes trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core platform functionality and app lifecycle operations.
  • Firebase Firestore: We utilize cloud caching mechanisms to store generalized numerological definitions and analyses. This data does not contain identifiable personal profiles.
  • Google AdMob: The app may use Google AdMob in future updates to help keep our services free. AdMob may automatically collect and use device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with applicable advertising policies.

3Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

4Data Security

We value your trust in providing numerical inputs for lifestyle and entertainment insights. We employ industry-standard technical measures and rely on Google Cloud's enterprise-grade infrastructure to protect all cached and transmitted analytical data.

5Changes to This Privacy Policy
